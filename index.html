About Sahel Agro

Building Reliable Connections Across the Agricultural Supply Chain

Sahel Agro Distribution Co. Ltd. is an agricultural distribution and supply company focused on connecting agricultural production with reliable markets.

We work across the supply chain by identifying market demand, sourcing agricultural products from farmers and suppliers, aggregating available supply and coordinating delivery to businesses and other customers.

Our approach is built around relationships, transparency, consistency and practical market access. Rather than simply moving products from one location to another, we aim to create dependable connections between those who produce agricultural goods and those who need them.

As the company grows, Sahel Agro will expand its capabilities across agricultural sourcing, distribution, processing and other areas of the agricultural value chain.

Focus Agricultural sourcing, aggregation and distribution
Approach Relationship-driven, transparent and consistent
Buyers Commercial, retail and institutional customers
Suppliers Farmers, producers and agricultural suppliers

We operate at the points where agricultural production meets commercial demand — sourcing, consolidating, coordinating and delivering.

01

We identify and source agricultural products according to customer requirements, working with farmers, producers and trusted suppliers.

02

Aggregation

03

Distribution

How It Works

From Demand to Delivery

A straightforward process designed around clarity — you tell us what you need, we handle the coordination from source to destination.

01

Tell Us What You Need

Submit your agricultural supply request, including the product, quantity, quality requirements and delivery location.

02

We Source

We identify suitable farmers, producers or suppliers who can meet your requirements.

03

We Aggregate

Where necessary, we consolidate supply from multiple producers to meet the required quantity.

04

We Coordinate

We confirm availability, specifications, pricing and delivery arrangements.

05

We Supply

Once everything is agreed, we coordinate delivery to the specified destination.

06

Build a Relationship

For recurring requirements, we work toward establishing a consistent supply relationship.
